Calendar colour

All matches use Liverpool red #dc0714.

Apple Calendar usually picks this up from the file. In Google Calendar a URL subscription uses the colour you choose: click the dots next to the calendar → pick a red (Tomato is closest).

Kick-off not yet confirmed

When a date is known but the time is still TBC (typical Saturday 3pm placeholders, or cup ties before the TV slot), the match appears as an all-day event with (kick-off TBC) in the title. It switches to a timed 2-hour event once the kick-off is confirmed.

How times update

A job runs every morning and again in the afternoon (06:00 and 16:00 UTC). It checks Wikipedia and other feeds for TV-confirmed kick-offs. When a time is confirmed, the all-day event becomes an exact UK kick-off (for example 12:30 or 17:45).

That change is on the website after the job finishes. Google Calendar often waits 12–24 hours to refresh a subscribed feed; Apple is usually faster. Download the file again anytime to see the latest times.

How it works

Fixtures are kept in the project and refreshed from official Liverpool FC, Premier League, and UEFA announcements. The scheduled job republishes the .ics file. Your calendar app picks up new fixtures, TV time changes, and results on its next refresh.
